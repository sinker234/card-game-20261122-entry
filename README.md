Ember Court — an original lane-battle card duel. You are the Ember Envoy, duelling Rook, the Clockwork Duchess, across three lanes (The Court, The Garden, The Vault). Play cards from your hand, outplay her suit for suit, and bring her 20 life to zero. Four suits told apart by colour and shape (Ember ▲, Tide ●, Leaf ◆, Star ★) with a suit cycle, Harmony combos, Lone Star bonuses, a readable AI that telegraphs the lane she is eyeing, and full win/loss screens. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best card game" bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Or serve the `game/` folder with any static server and open `game/index.html` — the game runs fully offline, no network requests. Play with mouse or keyboard: arrow keys pick a card and a lane, Enter plays, E ends the turn, R restarts, H shows the rules.
